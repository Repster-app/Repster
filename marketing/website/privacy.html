Repster Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Overview

Repster is a workout logging app for iOS. Workout history, exercises, templates, bodyweight entries, and settings are stored locally on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

Workout Data

Repster stores your workout data on device so the app can show history, charts, records, templates, and active workout context. Repster does not send exercise names, set weights, reps, notes, CSV contents, bodyweight values, or raw workout logs to product analytics.

Anonymous Product Analytics

Repster uses PostHog EU cloud for anonymous product analytics. Analytics help us understand aggregate app usage: which screens are opened, how onboarding progresses, when workouts are started, completed, discarded or abandoned, coarse ranges for things like workout duration and set counts, paywall and purchase actions, import completion, and backup export. Repster does not create accounts, and analytics are grouped under a random identifier generated on your device at install time.

Analytics are enabled by default and can be turned off in the app at Settings -> Data & Backups -> Share Anonymous Analytics. Turning this off disables event collection, session recordings, and surveys together.

Session Recordings

Repster records anonymous playback of app screens through PostHog session replay, so we can see where the app is confusing to use. All text is masked before a recording leaves your device — recordings show layout, navigation and where you tapped, with every label, number and text field obscured. Images are masked in the same way. This means recordings do not reveal exercise names, weights, reps, notes, bodyweight values, or any other workout content.

Recordings contain no network request contents and no app logs. They are covered by the same setting as the rest of analytics and stop entirely when you turn Share Anonymous Analytics off.

In-App Surveys

Repster occasionally shows short multiple-choice surveys asking about your experience with the app. Answering is always optional, responses are stored against the same anonymous device identifier, and Repster does not collect free-text responses through surveys.

Purchases

Repster uses RevenueCat to manage App Store purchases and entitlements. RevenueCat may process purchase history and anonymous app user identifiers to validate purchases, unlock paid features, prevent fraud, and provide purchase analytics.

What Repster Does Not Do

Repster does not use analytics for advertising, does not sell data, does not use IDFA, and does not enable PostHog autocapture or heatmaps. Repster has no user accounts, so no analytics data is linked to a name, email address, or any other real-world identity. Repster does not send exercise names, set weights, reps, notes, CSV contents, bodyweight values, or raw workout logs to analytics — including in session recordings, where all text is masked.

Support

If you contact Repster for support, we use the information you provide only to respond to your request.
