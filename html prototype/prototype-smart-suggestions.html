A few quick observations on the current design

  • The card has two titles ("SMART SUGGESTIONS" section header + "Smart Suggestions" card title) — the duplication eats space and makes the icon feel decorative.
  • "1 suggestion ready" is meta info; users care about what the suggestion is, not that one exists. Consider dropping it or shrinking it.
  • The 3-line description ("Based on your recent performance…") is long and shows up every time, even when the user already trusts the feature. A short "Why?" disclosure or a tooltip handles this with less weight.
  • There's no obvious action on the suggestion. Adding "Apply" / "Use" turns the card from passive info into something useful in a hurry.
