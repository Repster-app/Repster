My read

If I had to ship one, I'd go Take A:

  • Keeps the rail + icon character you liked.
  • One line per strip — 4 sets fit comfortably without dominating the screen.
  • Use button per strip is redundant with the keyboard rail, but as a visual affordance it makes the suggestions feel "actionable" rather than just informational. Worth the small duplication.
  • No engine work, no new copy generation.

Take B is the prettiest but the second per-row line is repetitive without contextual copy, which is a follow-up effort. Take C is the most minimal and the only one that fully respects the keyboard-rail-is-the-action principle — quietest, least personality.
