Model transparency & coaching

Ten sketches: two on model transparency, then eight coaching tiles across the domains a coach actually covers — what to train, load progression, session structure, scheduling, goals, stimulus variety and balance. Every one runs on data the app already stores. The constraints under each are the part worth arguing with.

The category is mostly a re-frame, not new machinery. Much of what ships is already coaching-adjacent — deloadReadiness, muscleBalance and restSweetSpot all imply an action without naming one. The line that would define a coaching tile is whether it names the next step. That makes this a grouping and copy-register decision first and an engine decision second.

Two separate gates. Coverage: the first two tiles read FatigueObservation, which only exists where Smart Suggestions produced a prediction — blank for anyone not using the wand, so neither can be Tier 1. Stance: four of the coaching tiles are marked “stance needed” because the finding is safe but the recommendation is an opinion the app has never yet asserted.

FatigueObservation carries prescribedWeight, actualWeight, predictedEffectiveE1RM, actualE1RM and normalizedError per set; FatigueLearningService.diagnosticsExercises() and recentSessionSummaries() already roll it up, and FatigueLearningAdminView renders a version of this today. free Already a first-class concept. Ranking defaults would show the user the model's priors dressed up as a fact about their training. Gate on source and hasAuditHistory, and show only learned rows.

free Different question from the muscle panel. That one asks how much; this asks how it's distributed — and 16 sets in one day is a different stimulus from 16 across three.
