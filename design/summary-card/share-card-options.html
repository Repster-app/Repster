The four values that were mine, not the app’s

The muscle colours were already right. These were sketch values that had crept in.

WasNow
#0E0E11the card ground → Color.bg #111113

One consequence worth seeing on a phone: the ground is now Color.bg, the same value as the app’s own screen, so the card sits a shade lighter than before and reads as a piece of Repster rather than as a separate object. That is what asking for the app’s palette buys, and it is easy to walk back if it looks too flat next to a photo in a story feed.
